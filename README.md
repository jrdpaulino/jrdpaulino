## Machine learning, AI and networking

My degree work centres on two things: how systems talk to each other, and how models learn from data.

**Machine learning and AI.** Coursework in Machine Learning, Neural Network Design and Testing, Probability and
Statistics and Game Theory, backed by projects I run and measure myself: an MLP against LeNet-5 on three image datasets
in PyTorch, and a layered Markov model of the market cycle that scores every one of its own forecasts. I care less
about a clever model than about whether it is tested honestly, which is why my trading research ends in a documented
"no edge found". I also work daily with AI coding tools (Claude Code, GitHub Copilot) and treat them as collaborators
whose output still has to be tested.

**Computer networking.** A Systems Networking focus, Computer Networks and Systems Programming at UT Dallas, plus
hands-on telecom work as a Nokia 5G RAN intern: a tool to create, read, update and delete the XML configuration that
brings 4G and 5G cells on-air, and a dashboard of live cell parameters. In the field I diagnose connection problems and
bring devices back onto real on-site networks.

## Music and medicine: a wider view of code

Years of piano, percussion, harp, and operatic singing in school and at working gigs taught me to listen before I act, keep time with other people, and the value of practice and repetition. Working as a nursing student on a busy medical-surgical unit, with up to 20 patients a shift and training to support code and crash teams, taught me to stay calm when something fails, triage what matters first, explain things plainly to worried people, and treat a checklist as a safety tool. Together they shape how I program: reading a system like a score, debugging like a clinician, and remembering that software is used by people in real situations and should be built with them in mind.

## Projects

**[Market Signal Conductor](https://github.com/jrdpaulino/market-signal-conductor)**: a web service that combines
SEC filings, prediction-market odds, macro data and news in a layered Markov model of the market cycle, then scores
every forecast against what the market did next. Python, SQLite, Fly.io, 61 tests, no third-party dependencies.

**[EDGAR Insider Copy-Trader](https://github.com/jrdpaulino/edgar-insider-copytrader)**: an SEC Form 4 insider-buying
agent with a risk gate, plus backtests with a holdout period. No edge found, and the write-up shows the numbers.

**[Machine Learning Models](https://github.com/jrdpaulino/Machine-Learning-Models)**: an MLP against LeNet-5 on MNIST,
Fashion-MNIST and CIFAR-10 in PyTorch, with a results table and one-click Colab notebooks.

**[Congress Copy-Trader](https://github.com/jrdpaulino/congress-copytrader)**: the first version of the trading
agent, kept to show how the work progressed. The story of the three trading repos is in
[`docs/PROGRESS.md`](https://github.com/jrdpaulino/edgar-insider-copytrader/blob/main/docs/PROGRESS.md).
